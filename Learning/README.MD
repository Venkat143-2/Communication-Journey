# 📚 Learning

The `Learning/` folder contains my **actual communication practice**.

This folder is different from the `Feedback/` folder.

> **Learning = What I actually practiced**
> **Feedback = What I need to improve**

I will not replace my original work with corrected versions. My original speaking, listening, and writing should remain here so I can compare my progress over time.

---

# 🎯 Purpose

The purpose of this folder is to maintain a record of my daily communication practice.

Every day I will spend approximately **30 minutes**:

```text
30 MINUTES
    │
    ├── 🎤 Speaking   → 10 minutes
    │
    ├── 🎧 Listening  → 10 minutes
    │
    └── ✍️ Writing    → 10 minutes
```

The practice should be consistent rather than perfect.

# 🎧 Listening

I will spend approximately **10 minutes every day listening to English**.

Possible sources:

* Podcasts
* YouTube videos
* Interviews
* Talks
* Discussions
* News
* Technical videos
* Educational content
* Conversations

The purpose is to improve:

* Listening comprehension
* Vocabulary recognition
* Pronunciation recognition
* Understanding natural speech
* Understanding different accents
* Understanding sentence patterns
* Understanding speaker intention
* Ability to follow conversations

---

## 📝 Listening Record

For each listening practice, I should record:

1. What I listened to
2. The source
3. Original transcript, when available
4. What I understood
5. Words/phrases I didn't understand
6. Questions or confusing parts

Example:

```markdown
# Week 01

## Day 01

### Content

**Title:** [Podcast/Video title]

**Source:** [YouTube / Podcast / etc.]

### Original Transcript

[Transcript of the section I listened to]

### What I Understood

[Write what I understood in my own words]

### Words/Phrases I Didn't Understand

- phrase 1
- phrase 2
- phrase 3

### Difficult Parts

[What was difficult to understand?]
```

---

## 🔄 Listening Process

```text
Listen
   ↓
Understand
   ↓
Write what I understood
   ↓
Compare with original transcript
   ↓
Identify differences
   ↓
Learn from mistakes
```

My goal is not to understand **100% immediately**.

The goal is to gradually improve my ability to understand natural English.

# 🧠 Reflection Writing

When writing about a difficult or uncomfortable situation, I can use:

```text
Situation
    ↓
What happened?
    ↓
What did I feel?
    ↓
Why did I feel that way?
    ↓
What did I think?
    ↓
What could I control?
    ↓
How could I communicate better?
    ↓
What will I do next time?
```

This makes writing useful for both **English improvement and self-reflection**.

The purpose is not simply to blame another person.

The purpose is to understand the situation and improve how I communicate and respond.

# 🔗 Relationship With Feedback

The `Learning/` folder stores the **original practice**.

The `Feedback/` folder stores the **analysis and corrections**.

```text
              ORIGINAL PRACTICE
                     │
        ┌────────────┼────────────┐
        ↓            ↓            ↓
     Speaking     Listening     Writing
        │            │            │
        └────────────┼────────────┘
                     ↓
                  FEEDBACK
                     ↓
              Identify mistakes
                     ↓
               Understand why
                     ↓
                  Improve
```

For example:

```text
Learning/Speaking/Week-01.md
              ↓
Feedback/Speaking/Week-01.md
```

```text
Learning/Listening/Week-01.md
              ↓
Feedback/Listening/Week-01.md
```

```text
Learning/Writing/Week-01.md
              ↓
Feedback/Writing/Week-01.md
```

# ⚠️ Important Rule

## Keep the original work.

If I make a grammar mistake while speaking or writing, **I should not silently correct it in the Learning folder**.

The mistake itself is valuable because it allows me to measure improvement later.

```text
Original
   ↓
Mistakes
   ↓
Feedback
   ↓
Correction
   ↓
Future Practice
```
