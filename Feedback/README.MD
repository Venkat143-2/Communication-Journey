# 📝 Feedback

The `Feedback/` folder contains feedback, corrections, observations, and improvement suggestions for the communication practice stored inside the [`Learning/`](../Learning/) folder.

The purpose of feedback is not just to identify mistakes.

The purpose is to understand:

> **What did I do wrong → Why was it wrong → How can I improve it → Can I use the improvement again?**

---

## 🎯 Purpose

Feedback helps track improvement in:

* 🗣️ Speaking
* ✍️ Writing
* 🎧 Listening
* 📚 Vocabulary usage
* 🧠 Clarity of thoughts
* 🔤 Grammar
* 🗣️ Pronunciation
* 🎵 Intonation and tone
* 💬 Conversation skills
* 🧩 Sentence formation
* 🎯 Audience awareness
* 💪 Confidence and delivery

The `Learning/` folder stores the **original practice**.

The `Feedback/` folder stores the **analysis and corrections**.

# 🗣️ Speaking Feedback

Speaking feedback focuses on how effectively ideas are communicated verbally.

### Things to evaluate

#### 1. Clarity

* Was the idea easy to understand?
* Did I explain things in the correct order?
* Did I jump between different ideas?

#### 2. Grammar

Look for:

* Incorrect tense
* Incorrect articles
* Subject-verb agreement
* Incorrect prepositions
* Incorrect sentence structure
* Missing words

#### 3. Vocabulary

Check:

* Incorrect word usage
* Repeated words
* Simple words where a better word would be useful
* New vocabulary used correctly
* Naturalness of expressions

#### 4. Pronunciation

Check:

* Mispronounced words
* Difficult sounds
* Word stress
* Sentence stress

#### 5. Delivery

Check:

* Speaking speed
* Pauses
* Tone
* Confidence
* Monotone speech
* Filler words such as:

  * "um"
  * "uh"
  * "like"
  * "actually"
  * "you know"

#### 6. Structure

A good spoken explanation should generally have:

```text
Start
  ↓
Main idea
  ↓
Explanation
  ↓
Example
  ↓
Conclusion
```

# ✍️ Writing Feedback

Writing feedback focuses on the ability to express thoughts clearly through written English.

### Things to evaluate

#### Grammar

Check:

* Tenses
* Articles
* Prepositions
* Pronouns
* Subject-verb agreement
* Sentence structure
* Conjunctions
* Punctuation

#### Vocabulary

Check:

* Word choice
* Repeated vocabulary
* Incorrect usage
* Natural expressions
* New vocabulary

#### Clarity

Ask:

> Can another person understand exactly what I am trying to say?

#### Organization

Check whether the writing has a logical flow:

```text
Idea
 ↓
Explanation
 ↓
Example / Situation
 ↓
Result
 ↓
Reflection
```

#### Natural English

The goal is not only grammatically correct English.

The goal is:

> **Clear + Natural + Appropriate English**

# 🎧 Listening Feedback

Listening feedback is different from speaking and writing feedback.

The main goal is to understand how well I can understand real English.

Track:

* Words I couldn't understand
* Words I heard incorrectly
* New vocabulary
* Expressions
* Pronunciation differences
* Fast speech
* Connected speech
* Different accents
* Main idea comprehension
* Details I missed

### Listening Analysis

After listening to something, record:

```text
Source:
Date:

What was the main idea?

What did I understand?

What did I miss?

New words:

Difficult expressions:

Pronunciation I noticed:

What should I improve?
```

# 📈 Track Repeated Mistakes

A mistake that happens once is normal.

A mistake that happens repeatedly needs special attention.

Create a section like:

```markdown
## 🔁 Repeated Mistakes

| Mistake | Count | Status |
|---|---:|---|
| Article "a/an/the" | 8 | Improving |
| Wrong preposition | 6 | Needs Practice |
| "is/are" agreement | 4 | Improving |
| Filler word "actually" | 12 | Needs Practice |
```

The goal is to reduce the number of repeated mistakes over time.

# 🧠 Feedback → Practice

Feedback is useful only when it changes future behavior.

Use this cycle:

```text
Practice
   ↓
Feedback
   ↓
Identify Mistake
   ↓
Understand Why
   ↓
Practice Correction
   ↓
Use It Again
   ↓
Feedback
```

For example:

```text
Mistake:
"I am knowing the answer."

Why:
"Know" is generally a stative verb and is not normally used
in the continuous form.

Correction:
"I know the answer."

Practice:
Create 5 new sentences using "know" correctly.
```

# ⭐ Strengths Matter Too

Feedback should not only focus on mistakes.

Record things that are improving.

For example:

```text
## ⭐ Strengths

- I explained my idea without stopping.
- My sentences were more organized.
- I used 3 new vocabulary words correctly.
- I used fewer filler words.
- My speaking speed was better.
```

This helps identify what is already becoming natural.

# 🚫 Important Rule

Do **not** silently modify the original work inside `Learning/`.

Keep:

```text
Original Practice
      +
   Feedback
      +
Improved Version
```

This creates a record of the journey.

After several months, I should be able to look back and see:

> **How I communicated before → What mistakes I made → What I learned → How I communicate now**

---

# 🎯 Main Goal

The purpose of feedback is not:

> ❌ "I made many mistakes."

The purpose is:

> ✅ "I know exactly what my mistakes are, why I make them, and what I need to practice to stop making them."

---

# 🔥 Golden Rule

> **Don't just collect corrections. Turn corrections into habits.**

```text
Mistake → Understand → Correct → Practice → Repeat → Improve
```

That is the real purpose of the `Feedback/` folder.
